Build a modern, responsive ONE-PAGE landing website for a web design and hosting service called **“Sergios Designs”**.

Overall goals:
- Tech-forward, sleek, minimal.
- HIGH-CONVERTING: push users to fill out the contact form.
- One pager only (no inner pages).
- NO phone number anywhere on the site.
- Include a contact form (name, email, company/website, project budget, message).

Brand vibe:
- Modern, clean, confident solo/freelance studio.
- “I’ll handle everything for your website – design, build, and hosting.”
- Tone: professional, straightforward, not corporate and not too playful.

Color & style:
- Dark, tech-forward theme with a subtle gradient background.
- Accent color: electric blue or teal for buttons and highlights.
- Use lots of whitespace, rounded cards, smooth hover states, and subtle shadow.
- Simple, readable sans-serif font (like Inter, Manrope, or similar).
- Smooth scrolling between sections and light fade/slide-in animations on scroll.

Layout & sections (in order):

1. HEADER (sticky)
   - Left: simple text logo: **Sergios Designs**.
   - Right: minimal nav with anchor links:
     - “Services”
     - “Portfolio”
     - “Process”
     - “About”
     - “Contact”
   - On the far right: primary button “Get a Free Quote” linking to the Contact section.
   - Header should be slim, with a translucent / blurred background on scroll.

2. HERO SECTION (kick-ass hero)
   - Full-width hero above the fold.
   - Left side content:
     - Small badge: “Website Design • Hosting • Care Plans”
     - Main headline (H1), bold and short:
       “Launch a Website That Actually Works for Your Business”
     - Supporting subheadline:
       “Sergios Designs builds fast, modern websites and handles hosting for you—so you can focus on running your business while your site does the selling.”
     - Primary CTA button: “Start Your Project”
        - Scrolls to Contact form.
     - Secondary ghost button: “View Portfolio”
        - Scrolls to Portfolio section.
   - Right side:
     - A clean mockup-style graphic or card stack that visually represents:
       - A website preview in a browser window
       - Analytics/graph overlay
       - A small hosting/server icon
     - This should feel techy and modern, not cheesy.

3. SOCIAL PROOF / HIGHLIGHTS BAR
   - Thin strip under hero with simple text icons or tags:
     - “Custom Design”
     - “Fast Hosting Included”
     - “Mobile-First”
     - “Ongoing Support Options”
   - Use minimal icons and subtle dividers.

4. SERVICES SECTION
   - Title: “Services”
   - Short intro sentence:
     “Everything you need to get online and stay online — in one place.”
   - Three or four service cards in a grid:
     1) “Custom Website Design”
        - Copy: “Bespoke designs tailored to your brand, optimized for conversion and mobile.”
     2) “Development & Launch”
        - Copy: “Clean, responsive builds with fast load times and modern best practices.”
     3) “Hosting & Care Plans”
        - Copy: “Managed hosting, backups, and updates so you never worry about downtime.”
     4) (Optional) “Optimization & Refresh”
        - Copy: “Need a revamp? I optimize existing sites for design, speed, and UX.”
   - Each card should have a small icon and a clean hover effect.

5. PORTFOLIO SECTION
   - Title: “Recent Work”
   - Subtitle:
     “A snapshot of projects designed to look great and convert.”
   - Grid of 3–6 portfolio items (use placeholders but design them like real projects):
     - Each item: screenshot thumbnail, project title, one-line description, and a tag like “Local Business Website”, “E-commerce”, “Landing Page”.
     - On hover: slightly scale up, show overlay with a short CTA like “View details”.
     - For now, clicking can open a simple modal/lightbox or just enlarge the image; no need for separate pages.

6. SIMPLE PROCESS SECTION
   - Title: “How It Works”
   - Three-step horizontal or vertical layout:
     1) **1. Discover**
        - “We define your goals, content, and what success looks like.”
     2) **2. Design & Build**
        - “You review polished design concepts while I handle the build and hosting setup.”
     3) **3. Launch & Support**
        - “We go live, then I stick around with care plans and optimizations if you need them.”
   - Add a small note under the steps:
     “Most projects move from first call to launch in just a few weeks.”

7. ABOUT SECTION
   - Title: “About Sergios Designs”
   - Layout: text on the left, simple avatar or abstract icon on the right.
   - Copy (short, tweakable):
     “I’m Sergio, a web designer and developer based in Florida, focused on building clean, conversion-driven websites for small businesses and growing brands. I handle design, development, and hosting, so you have one partner for your entire web presence.”
   - Include a small bullet list like:
     - “Responsive, mobile-first builds”
     - “Clear communication and quick turnarounds”
     - “Flexible hosting and support options”

8. CTA STRIP BEFORE CONTACT
   - Full-width band with a clear callout:
     Headline: “Ready for a website that brings in more business?”
     Subtext: “Tell me about your project and I’ll follow up with a plan and pricing.”
     Button: “Get a Free Quote” → scrolls to Contact.

9. CONTACT SECTION (main conversion area)
   - Title: “Start Your Project”
   - Short description:
     “Fill out the form below and I’ll get back to you with next steps, timeline, and a clear quote.”
   - Contact form fields:
     - Full Name (required)
     - Email (required)
     - Company / Website (optional)
     - Project Budget (dropdown with ranges like: “Under $1,500”, “$1,500–$3,000”, “$3,000+”)
     - Project Details (multi-line textarea with placeholder: “Tell me about your business, your goals, and what you need help with.”)
   - Submit button: “Send My Project Details”
   - Below form: tiny line of reassurance, e.g.:
     “No spam. No hard sales. Just an honest conversation about what you need.”
   - IMPORTANT: Do NOT show any phone number anywhere on this page.

10. FOOTER
    - Simple, minimal footer.
    - Left: “© [Current Year] Sergios Designs. All rights reserved.”
    - Right: Tiny text links:
      - “Privacy Policy”
      - “Terms”
    - Add smooth scroll-back-to-top icon or button.

Technical & UX notes:
- Fully responsive: hero and portfolio must look great on mobile and desktop.
- Smooth scroll for all in-page navigation links.
- Clear visual hierarchy: hero CTA is the most prominent; secondary CTAs support it.
- Make sure buttons and form fields are accessible, with good contrast and clear focus states.
- Optimize for fast load times (avoid bloated images).

Use compelling microcopy where appropriate and wire all anchor links correctly to scroll to their sections.
